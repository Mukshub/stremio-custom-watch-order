Custom Watch Order

Give each watch order a name. One show can have several (e.g. a "chaos" order and a "chronological" one). Install the link once — add or rename orders any time and they appear on every device (reopen the show to refresh).

Heads up: Stremio saves an addon's list of rows when you install it and doesn't check for changes, so new, renamed or reordered collections won't show up by themselves (restarting doesn't help). To see them, remove Custom Watch Order in Stremio and add it again with your install link above. Your orders and watch history stay. It will move to the bottom of your addon list though. Adding, editing or moving orders between existing collections shows up without any of this.
